Reading it

  • Pie size is total alkalinity released by the final year (or the dissolved fraction or exported share, from the switch). Slices are where that alkalinity sits at the end.
  • Ceiling binds when drainage cannot carry the alkalinity the rule wants to export. The rest stays in the soil, raises pH up to the saturation cap, then precipitates as calcite.
  • Q-tags mark the open questions in the Alison meeting note. Slide them to see which ones change the answer at a given site.
  • Simplified on purpose: one site mean (control sites average 5 cropland points), no exchange/porewater split, no acid inputs or secondary minerals, excess Mg never exported. Site means can differ slightly from the 5-point model runs.
  • Source: branch v4-irrigated-drainage, provenance entries C13–C14; tables built by scripts/export_explorer_data.py.
